Clinical safety contract

          
A Skill makes a workflow repeatable. It does not make ChatGPT a doctor.

          

            These free tools are designed to make safer behaviours visible:
            minimise data, distinguish evidence from inference, expose missing
            context and return the decision to a qualified clinician.
          

        

        

          

            Before
            
De-identify

            

              Remove names, numbers, exact dates of birth, addresses and any
              unnecessary patient detail.
            

          

          

            During
            
Ground

            

              Supply the actual record, protocol or evidence excerpt. Do not ask
              the model to fill gaps from confidence alone.
            

          

          

            After
            
Verify

            

              Check facts, units, sources, language and patient-specific
              implications before clinical or patient use.
            

          

        

        

          Not for emergencies or autonomous decisions.
          Do not use these tools to diagnose, prescribe, determine a dose,
            replace direct medical assessment or process identifiable patient
            data without an approved governance pathway.
