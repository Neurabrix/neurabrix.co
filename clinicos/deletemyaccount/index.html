What happens next

  1. 1
    Send the prepared email

    Your email app will open with the details you entered. Send it to complete the request.

  2. 2
    Verify your identity

    We may confirm account ownership or ask the relevant clinic to verify your request.

  3. 3
    Delete eligible data

    We will delete or de-identify the account and associated data that we are permitted to delete, and notify relevant service providers where required.

  4. 4
    Confirm completion

    We will tell you when the request is complete or explain any lawful retention that still applies.

Data covered

What we delete

Subject to verification, applicable law, and the instructions of the healthcare organization responsible for the data, deletion generally covers your ClinicOS account, access credentials or login associations, profile and preference data, and other personal data associated with your use of the app that is not required to be retained.

Limited retention

What may be retained

A clinic or healthcare provider may be legally required to retain medical records even after your app account is deleted. Neurabrix or the provider may also retain limited billing, transaction, security, fraud-prevention, audit, dispute, or legal-compliance records. Retained data remains protected and is used only for the applicable retention purpose. Residual copies may remain in protected backups until their normal deletion cycle completes.
