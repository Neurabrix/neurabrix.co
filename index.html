About us

Products and digital systems, built end to end.

Neurabrix brings product strategy, AI, software engineering, cloud delivery, and digital presence management together in one senior, hands-on team. Our founders bring more than 30 years of combined experience in software product development and consulting.

Our team has built challenging products across every layer of the technology stack—from search and recommendation engines to machine learning models, large language models, LLM fine-tuning, AI assistants, AI agents, desktop applications, and healthcare software.

We work from a clear definition of the problem through design, implementation, deployment, and continuous improvement. Every engagement begins with transparent scope, cost, and quality expectations, with direct access to the people doing the work.
