A food-first method

Sustainable beats spectacular.

  1. Start at home. Build around familiar local and South Indian foods where they fit.
  2. Make it workable. Match the plan to routines, preferences, training and health context.
  3. Add with judgement. Supplements are considered judiciously, when context and the relevant professional guidance support them.
