How the scientific comparison works

We hold the facts, key tasks and scoring criteria constant, then change a bounded group of experience variables. Concepts 4–6 create an exploitation-to-exploration gradient: familiar control, strategic evolution and bounded challenger.

1 · First-pass signal

Ask each reviewer which concept they would open first and why, before discussing details.

2 · Task-based review

Have representative users find the programme, registration, venue and the next action without coaching.

3 · Weighted decision

Score task success 25%, effort 15%, next-action clarity 20%, trust 20%, visual fit 10% and mobile fit 10%.
