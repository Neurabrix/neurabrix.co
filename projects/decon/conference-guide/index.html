About DECON

Built in Coimbatore.
Connected globally.

DECON began in 2014 as the Diabetes Summit, welcoming more than 400 registrations in its first edition. It has grown into an international meeting for endocrinologists, diabetologists, physicians and allied professionals. Its digital edition reached more than 8,000 registrations worldwide.

Founded by organising secretary Dr Suresh Damodharan, DECON is designed to translate clinical evidence into everyday practice through lectures, case discussions, workshops, oral presentations, posters and professional exchange.

Frequently asked questions

Good to know.

Who should attend DECON 2027?

Endocrinologists, diabetologists, physicians, postgraduate trainees, nurses, dietitians, podiatrists and allied healthcare professionals interested in diabetes and endocrine care.

What does registration include?

Access to the scientific programme, exhibition and conference networking opportunities. Contact the conference office for current registration categories and fees.

Can I present my work?

Yes. DECON welcomes oral and poster presentations. Contact the scientific team for categories, eligibility, submission format and deadlines.

Is CME accreditation available?

DECON 2027 is approved for 4 CME credit hours by the Tamil Nadu Medical Council.

Where can I get accommodation support?

Use the hotel shortlist above and contact the conference office for delegate assistance.
