DECON clinical community

Why DECON

A clinical forum built for useful conversations.

Established in 2014 as the Diabetes Summit, DECON has grown into an international forum for practical learning and professional exchange. Its digital edition recorded more than 8,000 registrations from around the world.

The 13th edition continues the Academy’s focus on current practice, thoughtful debate and connections that last beyond the conference.
