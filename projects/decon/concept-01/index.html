Why DECON

A clinical forum built for useful conversations.

Established in 2014 as the Diabetes Summit, DECON has grown into an international forum for practical learning and professional exchange. Its 2020 digital edition recorded more than 8,000 registrations from around the world.

DECON 2027 will continue the Academy’s focus on current practice, thoughtful debate and connections that last beyond the conference.
