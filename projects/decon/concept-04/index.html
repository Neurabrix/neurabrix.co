About DECON

A trusted meeting for clinical exchange.

DECON began in 2014 as the Diabetes Summit with more than 400 registrations. It has grown into an international forum for endocrinologists, diabetologists, physicians and allied professionals.

Its digital edition reached more than 8,000 registrations worldwide. The 13th edition continues that commitment to useful evidence, practical discussion and enduring professional connections.
